PashuPrahari (पशुप्रहरी) 🐾

Real-Time Syndromic Surveillance & Early Outbreak Detection System for Livestock

PashuPrahari is an intelligent livestock health monitoring and disease surveillance platform designed to empower "rural farmers, field veterinarians, and livestock department administrators" with early disease detection, AI-assisted symptom analysis, automated risk assessment, and geospatial outbreak monitoring.

The platform enables farmers to report animal health problems using "English, Hindi, Marathi, and code-switched/vernacular input", while providing veterinarians and administrators with centralized tools to monitor, investigate, and manage livestock health cases.

✨ Key Features

👨‍🌾 Farmer Reporting
- Simple livestock health reporting interface.
- Supports "English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Marathi (मराठी)".
- Supports natural, conversational and code-switched symptom descriptions.
- Voice-assisted symptom input using browser speech recognition.
- Captures:
  - Animal species
  - Number of affected animals
  - Deaths
  - Duration of illness
  - Vaccination status
  - Location
  - Observed symptoms
- GPS-based location detection and manual coordinate entry.

🤖 AI-Assisted Symptom Analysis
PashuPrahari uses "Google Gemini" to analyze the farmer's current symptom description.

The AI:
- Understands natural farmer language.
- Handles multilingual and code-switched input.
- Normalizes symptoms into concise standardized terms.
- Identifies multiple symptoms from a single report.
- Suggests plausible disease/condition differentials.
- Provides an explanation of the assessment.
- Provides practical recommendations.
- Acts as "decision support rather than a definitive veterinary diagnosis".

> AI-generated information does not replace professional veterinary examination or diagnosis.

📊 Automated Risk Assessment
A deterministic rules engine calculates a standardized risk score:
- 0–100 Risk Score
- Low
- Moderate
- High
- Critical

Risk assessment considers factors such as:
- Severity of symptoms
- Number of affected animals
- Deaths
- Duration
- Vaccination status
- Disease-related indicators

The risk score is generated by the backend rules engine rather than being calculated by the frontend.

🗺️ Geospatial Disease Monitoring
- Interactive map powered by "Leaflet and OpenStreetMap".
- Visualizes reported livestock health cases geographically.
- Displays case locations and risk distribution.
- Supports identification of potential disease clusters and outbreak areas.

👨‍⚕️ Veterinarian Portal

Veterinarians can:
- View assigned cases.
- Review farmer reports and symptoms.
- Examine AI-assisted analysis.
- Investigate reported cases.
- Add treatment/investigation information.
- Update case status.
- Monitor cases assigned to them.

🏛️ Administrator Portal

Administrators can:
- Monitor incoming livestock health cases.
- View overall risk distribution.
- Assign cases to veterinarians.
- Monitor cases geographically.
- Track case statuses.
- Manage the veterinary response workflow.

🚨 Outbreak Awareness

The system is designed to support early identification of potential outbreaks by combining:
- Individual symptom reports
- Number of affected animals
- Death counts
- Geographic location
- Risk scores
- Case clustering

This enables veterinary authorities to identify potentially concerning patterns earlier.

3. AI & Rules Engine (/API)

PashuPrahari uses a dual-layer analysis architecture.
Generative AI Layer
Google Gemini (gemini-3.6-flash)

Used for:
Natural language understanding
Symptom extraction
Symptom normalization
Possible condition identification
Explanation generation
Recommendation generation
Multilingual / vernacular understanding
Deterministic Risk Engine

A rule-based engine provides consistent risk scoring from:
Risk Score: 0–100

with priority levels:
LOW
MODERATE
HIGH
CRITICAL

This separates AI-assisted clinical interpretation from deterministic risk prioritization.

             ↓

             ↓
3. Backend receives report
             ↓
4. Gemini analyzes current symptoms
             ↓
5. Symptoms are normalized
             ↓
6. Possible conditions generated
             ↓

             ↓
8. Case stored in MongoDB Atlas
             ↓
9. Case appears on Vet/Admin dashboards
             ↓
10. Veterinarian investigates
             ↓
11. Case status is updated
